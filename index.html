CPS Click Test

Clicks Per Second (CPS) is a measure of your manual speed. Gamers use this to gain advantages in combat systems.

How to Use: Click the test area as fast as possible. The 5-second countdown begins on your first click. Results are calculated instantly.

Polling Rate Test

Polling rate is how often your mouse reports its position to your PC. Higher Hz means smoother, more accurate tracking.

How to Use: Move your mouse in rapid circles within the test zone. The tool measures the micro-delays between reports to find your Hz frequency.

Scroll Wheel Audit

Hardware encoders in scroll wheels can degrade. This test detects if your wheel is skipping steps or sending ghost inputs.

How to Use: Scroll your wheel slowly to check individual notches, then quickly to test for speed-induced skipping.

Mouse & Keyboard Test

Ensure every switch on your peripheral is working. Useful for detecting dead keys or broken side buttons.

How to Use: Press keys or mouse buttons. The visual indicator will light up if the computer registers the electrical signal.
